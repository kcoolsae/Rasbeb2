After the contest
===
{% include _menu_en.md %}

As soon as the official contest is over, scores and answers are available. Pupils can
view their results when they log in, and teachers can see and download an
overview list:

* Under *Home* (Events) → *Details*, you see the results for that event
* Under *Contests* → *...* (button) → *Participations* (menu), you see the results per class, for all events together

In both cases you can also download the results as an Excel file.

Under *Contests* → *...* (button) → *Questions* (menu) you will now also see the correct answer for each question, and how
to arrive at this answer. There is also (almost)
always a few words of explanation about what the task has to do with
computer science (and sometimes, computational thinking).

(It would be nice if, as a teacher, you could find the time some day to go over those answers
with your pupils as well and to dwell here and there
on the computer science aspects of the question.)

#### Hiding fake participations

We ask you to *hide* so-called 'fake' participations within two weeks after the end of the official contest,
so as not to 'falsify' the statistics. A participation can be 'fake', for example because
- you took part yourself to test the contest,
- a pupil did the contest independently at home, and you suspect they had help,
- a pupil took part at a time when this was not intended, because the event was already/still open,
- …

To *hide* participations, go to the *Contests* screen, click the '...' button next to this year's official contest
and choose *Participations* in the submenu under the contest title. You will see a list of all
pupils who took part in the contest, per class. Click the button with the crossed-out eye to hide
that pupil's participation. 

#### Other ways to clean up the data 
You can change a pupil's name, because of spelling mistakes, or because you used another pupil's account
for a pupil.

- You can do this in the pupil overview (menu option *Pupils*), by clicking the pencil icon to the right of
  the pupil's name.
- Note, by the way, that for an event that is open you can still quickly add a new pupil, who can then take part in the event
  right away. (On the event's detail page click *Edit permissions*, 
  and then *Subscribe a new pupil*.)

You can delete an event that you have not used - as long as nobody has taken part in it yet. (This of course also
deletes all subscriptions for that event.)
- There is a red button *Delete event* at the top of an event's detail page.
