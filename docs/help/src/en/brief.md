# In brief: organizing the Bebras Challenge in your class
See also
* [Documentation - main page](index.html)

To organize the Bebras Challenge at your school, you have to go through the steps we describe below.
It is best to do most of this well in advance. (The contest itself takes place in November, during the week of 11 November and the week after.)

#### Logging in to the contest server. 
For this you need a user name and password. You can get them
in two ways:
   * Request them by e-mail from the Bebras coordinator for Flanders. Say which school you work at and what
your e-mail address is as a teacher at that school.
   * Have yourself registered by a colleague *from the same school* who already has an account. 

Your user name is your school e-mail address. You choose a password yourself.
You log in (for Belgium) via [this link](https://bebras.ugent.be/admin)[^1]. Put it in your *bookmarks*! 

#### Creating classes and registering pupils
See also
* [Registering classes and pupils](registration.md)
* [Menu overview](whatswhere.md)

You have to specify which *classes* will take part in the contest(s). You can choose the class names yourself,
    but it is best to use a short name, such as `3a`, `5-ib`. Not every pupil in every class has to
    take part in the contest. Pupils who do not take part do not have to be registered with the server, by the way.

After that you have to register the pupils. The easiest way to do this is by uploading an Excel file with the pupils' details
to the server. As
    part
    of this process the pupils are assigned a Bebras ID and a password, with which they can log in to the server.

#### Events
See also
* [Events](events.md)

To take part in a Bebras Challenge with your pupils, you have to schedule one or more *events*.
Suppose, for example, that on Thursday you want class 3a to take part in the
official contest, and on Friday class 5b. Then you create *two* events:

* A first one, of level *Padawan*[^3], which you name, for example, *Thursday 3a*.
* A second one, of level *Jedi*, which you then name *Friday 5b*.
        
Both events are linked to the same Bebras Challenge, but take place at a different
time.

After that you have to give the pupils *permission* to take part in one of those events.
You can give a whole class permission for an event - for example class 3a for *Thursday 3a* - but you can also
adjust those permissions individually per pupil - for example, all of 3a plus Jürgen from 3b.

#### How an event proceeds

You have to *open* the event just before it starts. Pupils can only take part in the contest
after this has been done. At the end of the event you have to close it. Only then can the
pupils see their results.

An *official* (international) contest can only be opened during the official contest weeks
(in November). And although you can (and must) close (an event for) an official contest
immediately after it has ended, pupils will only be able to
view their results *after* the official contest week.

#### Other contests

Once the official Bebras weeks are over, you can no longer create events for the official contest. Every
year (in July/August), however, a copy of the Bebras Challenge is made as a so-called *restricted* contest (= school contests). You can
create events for such school contests as you please during the year. Some teachers use
these contests as practice material for their pupils *before* the actual Bebras Challenge begins.

#### Footnotes
[^1]: Pupils use a [different link](https://bebras.ugent.be/contest).

[^3]: In Belgium we denote the age groups with names from the Star Wars stories. See [overview](overview.html#age-groups).
